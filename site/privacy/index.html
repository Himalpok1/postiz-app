Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Castrapid (“we”) is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at support@castrapid.com. When you upload content about other people, you decide why and how that data is used, and we process it for you.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, company name, time zone and a protected form of your password.
  • Connected channels: the access tokens and profile details a platform gives us when you connect it, such as your account name and picture.
  • Your content: the posts, images, videos, tags and schedules you create, and the performance figures platforms report for them.
  • Billing details: your plan, subscription status and invoices. Stripe handles your card, and we do not store the full card number.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device details, logs, and error reports.

3. How we use it

  • To provide Castrapid, including publishing your posts and showing analytics. This is needed to perform our contract with you.
  • To take payments and keep required records. This is needed for the contract and for legal duties.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it. These are our legitimate interests.
  • To send account and service messages, and marketing only where you have agreed or the law allows it. You can opt out of marketing at any time.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

  • The platforms you connect, when you ask us to publish or fetch data.
  • Stripe, for payments.
  • Service providers that host our systems and store media, process video, deliver email and monitor errors. They may use your data only to provide those services to us.
  • Authorities or advisers, where the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if the business is sold or reorganized.

Some providers are outside your country. Where the law requires it, we use recognized safeguards for those transfers.

5. Google and YouTube data

Castrapid’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Castrapid uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s Privacy Policy applies. You can remove our access at any time on your Google security settings page. We use this data only to publish your videos and show their analytics, and we do not share it with others except as needed to provide those features.

6. Removing a connected account

To stop Castrapid using a connected account, including Facebook or Instagram, disconnect the channel in Castrapid. To cancel the access itself, also remove Castrapid in that platform’s own settings.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while your account is active. Deleting your account in settings closes it and ends your sign-in and team access, but content such as posts and uploaded media can stay in our systems afterward.

To have your data erased, email support@castrapid.com. We will delete it and confirm once it is done. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, such as tax and billing records, and for as long as needed to resolve disputes.

8. Cookies

We use cookies that keep you signed in and remember your organization and layout, and your language and theme choices. Stripe, our payment provider, also sets cookies to help prevent fraud. These are needed for Castrapid to work.

If we add analytics or advertising cookies, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it. You can block cookies in your browser, though signing in needs them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or limit how we use it. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it. To use these rights, email support@castrapid.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your API keys private.

11. Children

Castrapid is not for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app.

13. Contact

Questions? Email support@castrapid.com.
