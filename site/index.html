Questions, answered.

What counts as a channel?

One connected account or page. An Instagram account and a Facebook page are two channels.

How does the trial work?

You get 7 days, and a card is required. It is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel first.

Is the code public?

Yes. Castrapid is open source under AGPL-3.0, and you can read the source code.
